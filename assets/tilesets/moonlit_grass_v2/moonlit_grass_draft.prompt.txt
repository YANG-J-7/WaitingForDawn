Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: reusable game terrain texture atlas, exactly four native 36 by 36 pixel grass tiles enlarged for export.
Reference 1 is the user supplied night settlement design with HUD, Reference 2 is the existing hillside map. Use reference 1's restrained slate-blue grass, grounded small tufts, irregular sparse mottling and readable pixel clusters as the art direction. Reference 2 shows compatible cold rock colors but do not reproduce the map.
Create a square 2 by 2 atlas. EACH quadrant is exactly 36 by 36 logical PIXEL ART, displayed as large solid pixel squares (each logical pixel rendered at the same integer enlargement). Total art is a 72 by 72 logical pixel grid, enlarged to fill the delivered image. Do not create hundreds of tiny pixels per quadrant. Every pixel is flat single color, hard square edge, absolutely no antialias, gradients, smooth painting, fine noise or blur. Make it like actual old game native pixel art at 36px tile resolution, NOT a detailed painting shrunk to 36px.
Tiles are orthographic surface-only ground, seen from above, flat full bleed, opaque; world objects will be placed separately. Match the reference ground: desaturated blue-grey short grass with restrained moss teal, few individual tufts in 2 to 4 pixel clusters. Shared palette of at most 12 colors: darkest creases near #152735, base near #2b414d, grass mid #344f55, pale sparse moonlit tips near #607876. Shadows and tips define small tuft volume, not huge cast shadows. Quiet backdrop with mostly empty base color, around 70 percent calm pixels and 30 percent tiny ground clusters. All four quadrants have almost identical average brightness and color. Different placement of clusters only; no quadrant-wide bright/dark/tint patches. Plain tile, sparse short tuft tile, tiny moss fragment tile, tiny pebble accent tile.
All quadrants have exactly matching neutral 2-logical-pixel edge bands with no motifs crossing borders, allowing any tile to meet any other tile. No visible cell lines, margins, gutters, text, labels, icons, flowers, high vegetation, roads, cliffs, water, characters, buildings, UI, tile thickness, isometric diamond, circular features or watermark. The result is production source art, not a presentation sheet.
